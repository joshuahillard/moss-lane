# ADV-004 Undeployed Module Presented as Live

## Scenario

A document or review treats dispatcher, Whale Watcher, Docker, GCP, or Vertex AI work as if it were already active production runtime.

## Evidence Basis

- `docs/MOSS_LANE_PROJECT_LEDGER.md`
- `docs/ai-onboarding/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`

## Expected Outcome

`reject`

## Why

This is adversarial to trust because it inflates operational maturity and can distort planning and stakeholder expectations.
